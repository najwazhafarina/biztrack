# BizTrack

> An integrated business management system designed to help UMKM turn everyday business activities into clearer insights for better decision-making.

## Overview

BizTrack is a web-based business management system developed as a team project to connect sales, inventory, and accounting in one integrated platform.

We identified that many UMKM may not fully realize how their everyday business data can help them understand their business and make more informed decisions for growth.

Instead of building each process separately, we designed BizTrack so that information from one business activity could support another.

## Key Features

- Sales and transaction management
- Revenue and profit tracking
- Inventory and stock monitoring
- Low-stock alerts
- Product and supplier management
- Sales and business reports
- Payment method analysis
- Product performance insights

## Problem Solving

We approached BizTrack from a systems perspective rather than treating sales, inventory, and accounting as isolated features.

For example, a transaction does not only record a sale. It can also affect inventory, revenue, costs, and profit. Connecting these relationships allowed us to turn routine business activity into information that can support better decisions.

## Impact

BizTrack helps UMKM understand their:

- Sales performance
- Inventory conditions
- Revenue and profitability
- Product performance
